# SunCoast-Retail-Visual-Analysis
Module 11 Assignment: Data Visualization with Matplotlib
### Purpose of this Program
This program is designed to create several visualizations based off of a given data set for a fictional company. Creates a variety of matplotlib visualizations to provide the firm with key business insights.
### Overview of Program Uses
1. Provides sales trends using time series visualization
2. Uses categorical comparison to show product performance by location
3. Analyzes relationships between advertising and sales
4. Customer distribution analysis
5. Sales distributions by pricing tiers
6. Market share analysis
### Notes to Mention
Requires installation of pandas, numpy, and matplotlib.
Generates several matplotlib visualizations.
Created as part of an educational assignment; should be reproducible using other data sets.
### Deeper Look at Program
1. Creates a line graph depicting total sales across each quarter
2. Makes a multi-line chart with sales trends for each location
3. Creates a grouped bar chart comparing category performance by location
4. Creates a stacked bar chart to show sales composition by location
5. Creates a scatter plot, including a line of best fit and outlier annotation, which analyzes the relationship between advertising spending and sales
6. Creates line chart showing sales per dollar spent on ads, including annotations on low and high efficiency
7. Creates two histograms show customer age distribution, and then age distribution by location (includes labels for mean and median ages)
8. Creates a boxplot showing purchases per age group
9. Creates a histogram that shows the number of customers per purchase amounts
10. Creates a pie chart that shows the sales by price tier, including percentages and highlighting the largest slice
11. Creates a pie chart showing the sales by category, including percentages and highlighting the largest slice
12. Creates a pie chart showing sales by location
13. Creates a comprehensive dashboard of the most relevant visualizations
